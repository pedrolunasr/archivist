SUPERNATURAL ARCHIVES DEPARTMENT

RECORDS DIVISION

INCIDENT REPORT

CASE FILE NO. 1889-3550

DATE: JUNE 09, 1889
LOCATION: New Orleans, Luisiana
REPORTING OFFICER: The Journalist
CLASSIFICATION: Cult activity/Murder

INCIDENT DESCRIPTION

Between 1884 and 1889, approximately thirty murders were reported across five states, all exhibiting a remarkably similar modus operandi. After conducting surveillance on a known cult member in New York, I uncovered evidence suggesting that the individual responsible for these killings was connected to certain occult activities. Given the unnatural circumstances surrounding the victims' deaths, I have reason to believe that the perpetrator may not be entirely human.

Upon arriving in New Orleans, I assumed the identity of a journalist in order to gain access to the local police department's records. During my inquiries, I attracted the attention of a police officer identified as John and an individual known as Fred, who subsequently began following me. After a confrontation within the police precinct, I managed to evade both men. Recognizing that my identity had been compromised, I adopted a new disguise and continued my investigation. My inquiries eventually led me to a library in downtown New Orleans, which the suspected murderer was known to frequent. Several books concerning forbidden rituals had apparently been left there deliberately, possibly as a means of attracting and recruiting individuals into the cult. Interestingly, John and Fred appeared to have uncovered connections of their own. Through their actions, they inadvertently revealed information that proved invaluable to my investigation.

The evidence ultimately led me to identify the coroner of St. Mary's Hospital as the individual responsible for the murders. Disguised as a physician, I prepared to confront the suspect. However, I encountered John and Fred once again before I could proceed. During the confrontation, I sustained a gunshot wound to the leg, leaving me unable to continue the investigation. Nevertheless, the two men proceeded to pursue the coroner. Several minutes later, I observed the aftermath of their confrontation. The coroner's office had been left in a state of considerable destruction. The suspect was confirmed deceased. Subsequent observations indicated that the coroner had undergone a grotesque physical transformation, assuming characteristics resembling those of a large arachnid. Whatever the true nature of this creature, its death has brought an end to the killings attributed to it.

OFFICER'S OBSERVATIONS

Despite the circumstances, I managed to gather additional information from John, Fred, and a journalist accompanying them, identified as America. Their accounts revealed several matters worthy of further investigation.

First, they described the existence of an unidentified entity capable of manifesting only in complete darkness. According to their testimony, the creature possessed a pair of unusually bright eyes. Second, evidence suggested the emergence of a newly established cult operating within New Orleans. The individuals involved reported that this organization had since been dealt with. Finally, I have reason to suspect that Fred obtained one of the ritualistic books previously belonging to the deceased coroner. The whereabouts of this volume remain unknown, and its potential significance should not be underestimated.

The Journalist
