PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

The subject appears incapable of manifesting in environments exposed to direct illumination, seemingly existing exclusively within shadows and areas of complete darkness. Consequently, no reliable physical description has been obtained, apart from a pair of pale, luminous eyes seemingly suspended in the darkness.

Witnesses consistently report these eyes appearing approximately 15 feet (4.5 meters) above ground level, suggesting that the entity may possess an unusually large physical form. Despite its unseen appearance, the subject has demonstrated considerable physical strength, reportedly capable of lifting and throwing a fully grown adult with little apparent effort.

OBSERVED BEHAVIOR

The subject appears capable of instantaneous displacement, possibly through teleportation or temporary withdrawal from physical reality. To date, no evidence suggests that the entity can manifest in multiple locations simultaneously.

Observations indicate that the subject deliberately pursues specific individuals or groups. The criteria by which it selects its victims remain entirely unknown, as does the extent of its ability to locate and track them.

The entity demonstrates pronounced hostility toward its chosen targets and possesses the apparent ability to communicate verbally. Several accounts describe the subject engaging its victims in conversation, frequently employing threats, mockery, and psychological intimidation during attacks.

Exposure to sufficient illumination appears to prevent the entity from manifesting. However, attacks have reportedly resumed immediately following the loss of a light source. Of particular concern is the subject's demonstrated ability to interfere with sources of illumination, including electrical lighting and other forms of artificial light. The precise nature and limitations of this ability remain undetermined.

INVESTIGATION NOTES

Available evidence remains insufficient to establish the subject's true nature, origin, or ultimate intentions. However, previous encounters suggest a possible connection to the Outer God known as "Nyarlathotep." This hypothesis is supported by reports of the entity's apparent association with cult activity documented in both New Orleans and Missouri. Whether the subject acts as a servant, manifestation, or independent agent of this entity remains unknown.

Personnel encountering the subject are strongly advised to maintain at least two independent, sufficiently powerful flashlights capable of illuminating an enclosed room. Continuous illumination should be maintained whenever possible, particularly in confined spaces or during nighttime hours.

Should the entity begin exhibiting hostile behavior, investigators are advised to remain within illuminated areas and avoid direct confrontation. Available observations suggest that the subject may eventually abandon its pursuit if denied an opportunity to manifest. However, this behavior has not been consistently verified, and illumination must not be regarded as an absolute guarantee of safety.

All encounters, sightings, or suspected manifestations of the subject must be reported immediately to the appropriate superior. Under no circumstances should personnel attempt to investigate the entity alone or share any information with him.
