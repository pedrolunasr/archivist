ARKHAM POLICE DEPARTMENT

MASSACHUSETTS — RECORDS DIVISION

INCIDENT REPORT

CASE FILE NO. 1926-001

DATE: October 17, 1926
LOCATION: West Street, Arkham
REPORTING OFFICER: Officer James Sullivan
CLASSIFICATION: Unexplained Occurrence

INCIDENT DESCRIPTION

At approximately 11:45 PM, officers responded to reports of unusual activity near an abandoned residence on West Street.

Upon arrival, the premises were found vacant. However, several unidentified markings were discovered on the interior walls.

Witnesses reported hearing unidentified sounds originating from the basement of the property. Further investigation is recommended.

OFFICER'S OBSERVATIONS

No signs of forced entry were identified. The investigation remains ongoing.

J. Sullivan
REPORTING OFFICER
