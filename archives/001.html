SUPERNATURAL ARCHIVES DEPARTMENT

RECORDS DIVISION

INCIDENT REPORT

CASE FILE NO. 1921-4178

DATE: DECEMBER 22, 1921
LOCATION: Bradleyville, Missouri
REPORTING OFFICER: The Archivist
CLASSIFICATION: Unexplained Occurrence

INCIDENT DESCRIPTION

At approximately 10:00 AM on December 22, a series of bodies were discovered throughout the small town of Bradleyville, Missouri. Most of the victims appeared to have been killed under ritualistic circumstances. Following a thorough investigation, the evidence led me to Blackwood Mansion, which appears to be the epicenter of the supernatural disturbances reported throughout the area.

Upon arrival, the premises appeared to be deserted. However, extensive signs of a violent struggle were observed throughout the residence, including scorch marks, bloodstains, and severely damaged furniture. Several unidentified markings were also discovered on the interior walls.

Further examination of the mansion's basement revealed evidence of experiments conducted by a German scientist whose identity remains unknown. Subsequent inquiries confirmed that several individuals had survived the incident, including an FBI agent identified only as Fred. Records indicate that this same individual was previously associated with cult-related activities in New Orleans in 1889, from which he also reportedly emerged alive.

OFFICER'S OBSERVATIONS

Neither Agent Fred nor the other identified survivors have provided substantial information regarding the events at Blackwood Mansion. The investigation remains ongoing. Current evidence suggests the involvement of a cult devoted to the Outer God known as "Nyarlathotep," an entity whose name has surfaced repeatedly in previous investigations involving unexplained phenomena. Of the entities documented thus far, this particular subject appears to be among the most active and persistent.
